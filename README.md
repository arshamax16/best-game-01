Embergarden is a luminous arcade survival game: you are Luma, a firefly keeper of the last lantern in a dark garden. Gather golden embers to feed your light, chain fireflies for combos, dodge the shadow wisps that hunger for your glow — and when the eighth wave breaks, banish the Moth King and bring back the dawn.

Eight waves, each with its own weather: gloomspores wake on wave 2 and spit slow orbs, wave 3 rains embers, wave 4 swarms with wisps, wave 5 blooms with fireflies worth double, wave 6 brings the gloom tide, wave 7 is the long night where the dark drinks faster. Moonpetals bloom now and then — one grants a shield that swallows a hit whole. Between waves the garden offers a gift: eight upgrades, from Wide Glow to Ember Trail. The Moth King telegraphs every swoop with a burning line, and only a dash through its resting glow can banish it — three times, and dawn breaks.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Move with the mouse or WASD / arrow keys, dash with Space or click, pause with P, mute with M.
